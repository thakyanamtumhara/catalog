Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of DTF printing at Sale91?

₹155 per metre (100 cm) of sheet length, plus 18% GST and delivery. The sheet is 22.8 in (57.9 cm) wide and you pay only for the length you use: 50 cm costs ₹77.50 and 2 metres cost ₹310 before GST.

What sheet size do you print?

Every sheet is 22.8 in (57.9 cm) wide. A sheet can be any length up to 390 in.

What file do I need to upload?

PNG or PDF with a transparent background, 250 DPI or more. Upload a ready 22.8 in wide sheet, or upload single designs and choose their width and pieces: the website sets every piece on the sheet for you.

Is there a minimum order?

There is no minimum length: a sheet can be as short as 1 in. To check the print quality first, order 1 metre for ₹155 plus GST and delivery.

How do I order DTF prints?

Order on dtf.bulkplaintshirt.com: upload your sheet or designs, enter the pieces, choose delivery and pay online.

Where are DTF prints dispatched from?

From Surat, by courier across India. Inside Surat, same-day bike delivery is available.

Is GST included in the price?

No. ₹155 per metre is before GST. 18% GST is added on the print and the delivery, and you get a GST bill.
