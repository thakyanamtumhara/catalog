What does 430 GSM brushed loopknit mean?

GSM is the weight of the fabric in grams per square metre. A higher number means a thicker, heavier fabric. Everyday T-shirts are usually 160–180 GSM, so a 430 GSM hoodie is very heavy and warm.

Loopknit has small loops on the inside of the fabric. In brushed loopknit those loops are brushed up, so the inside feels soft like fleece and holds more warmth. This fabric is 88% cotton, 12% polyester.

In a drop-shoulder hoodie the shoulder seam sits lower, on the upper arm, and the body is cut wider. That gives the relaxed, boxy streetwear look, and the heavy fabric helps it keep that shape.
