What is sublimation printing?

Sublimation is a heat-press printing method. The design is printed on sublimation paper with sublimation ink and then pressed onto the T-shirt. The heat turns the ink into a gas that bonds with the polyester fibres, so the print becomes part of the fabric instead of a layer on top.

Because there is no layer on top, the print does not crack or peel, and the printed area feels the same as the rest of the T-shirt. Sublimation suits full-colour designs and photos, sports and team jerseys, school and event T-shirts, and all-over prints.

Before you print

  • Polyester only. Sublimation ink bonds with polyester. On cotton it has nothing to hold on to, so the print fades or washes out. This T-shirt is cotton-feel polyester.
  • White or very light fabric. Sublimation inks are see-through and there is no white ink, so colours only come out true on a white or very light base. This T-shirt is white.
  • Heat press settings. Use the time, temperature and pressure your sublimation paper and ink maker recommends. Pre-press the T-shirt for a few seconds first to remove moisture and creases.
  • Test first. Print your design on one sample piece before a bulk run.
