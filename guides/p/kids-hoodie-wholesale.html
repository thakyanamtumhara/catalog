What does 320 GSM brushed loopknit mean?

GSM is the weight of the fabric in grams per square metre. A higher number means a thicker, heavier fabric. Everyday T-shirts are usually 160–180 GSM, so a 320 GSM hoodie is a warm winter weight.

Loopknit has small loops on the inside of the fabric. In brushed loopknit those loops are brushed up, so the inside feels soft like fleece and holds more warmth. This fabric is 88% cotton, 12% polyester.

Printing on kids hoodies

  • DTF: works on cotton and cotton-polyester fabric like this one. We print DTF sheets too: order DTF prints. They come as a separate parcel from the hoodies.
  • Screen printing: the thick, smooth outside of the fabric takes front and back prints well.
  • Embroidery: heavy fabric holds stitches well, good for small chest logos and names.

Kids sizes have a smaller print area, so size your design for the chest. Always test your print on one sample piece before a bulk run.
