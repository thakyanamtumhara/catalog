What does 240 GSM mean?

GSM is the weight of the fabric in grams per square metre. A higher number means a thicker, heavier fabric. Everyday T-shirts are usually 160–180 GSM; 240 GSM is a heavyweight fabric.

For an oversized drop-shoulder T-shirt the weight matters a lot. Heavier fabric falls straight and keeps the wide, boxy shape instead of clinging to the body. It is also less see-through in light colours like White, Off-white and Beige, and it feels more premium in the hand — which is why many streetwear brands choose 220–260 GSM.

Printing on 240 GSM

  • DTF — works on 100% cotton. We print DTF sheets too: order DTF prints. They come as a separate parcel from the T-shirts.
  • Screen printing — the thick, stable fabric gives a smooth base for plastisol and water-based inks.
  • DTG — direct-to-garment printing gives its best colours on 100% cotton.
  • Embroidery and puff print — heavier fabric holds stitches and raised prints better than a thin tee.

Always test your print on one sample piece before a bulk run.
